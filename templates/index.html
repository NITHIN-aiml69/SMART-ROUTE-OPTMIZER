Mathematical & Algorithmic Foundations

Structural Integrity Verified

1. Handshaking Property

Theorem: \(\sum_{v \in V} \deg(v) = 2|E|\). Every undirected corridor contributes exactly +1 to the degrees of both endpoints, guaranteeing that the degree sum is always even.

2. Planarity & Euler's Formula

Formula: \(V - E + F = 2\). For simple planar graphs \(E \le 3V - 6\). Ensures that pipeline corridors can be laid without physical crossover clashes.

3. Multi-Modal Dijkstra Engine

Complexity: \(\mathcal{O}((V + E) \log V)\) using a Min-Priority Queue. Computes time-optimal routes across diverse transit modes.

4. Minimum Spanning Tree

Strategy: Eliminates redundant loops while ensuring 100% connectivity. Kruskal's sorts globally (\(\mathcal{O}(E \log E)\)), Prim's grows from root (\(\mathcal{O}(E \log V)\)).

5. Hierholzer's Eulerian Tour

Condition: Traverses every street exactly once if degrees are all even (circuit) or exactly two are odd (trail) in linear \(\mathcal{O}(E)\) time.

6. Hamiltonian Patrol Cycle

Condition: Visits every designated intersection exactly once and returns to base, satisfying Dirac's sufficient condition (\(\deg(v) \ge |V| / 2\)).
